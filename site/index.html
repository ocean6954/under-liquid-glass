What it costs

Three things you give up. Decide before you install.

  • The collapsing address bar

    The document never scrolls, so in portrait the bars stay expanded. Landscape is unaffected.

  • Your document structure

    Every page needs a scroller and a surface, and scroll handling points at the scroller rather than the window.

  • A guarantee

    It rests on undocumented Safari compositing that can change in any iOS release. It folds back to a normal page when it stops working.
